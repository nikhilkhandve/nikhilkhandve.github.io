About Nikhil

Nikhil Khandve is an Indian author and writer. His debut novel, A Forgotten Purse, is a contemporary romance set against the backdrop of Mumbai.

His writing explores young love, ordinary moments, memory, loss, and the ways people carry the past with them.

A Forgotten Purse

Contemporary Romance  |  By Nikhil Khandve

Ishaan never expected an ordinary day in Mumbai to become the beginning of his entire world.

Aadhya was the girl who forgot her purse at McDonald's. Ishaan was the boy who remembered her.

What followed was made of ordinary things: school corridors, coffee after class, walks through Mumbai, Marine Drive evenings, and six sunflowers bought at a flower market. Somewhere between all those small moments, friendship became something more.

They were young enough to believe they had time.

But some love stories are changed by things neither person can control. And when the future they imagined begins slipping further away, Ishaan has to learn that loving someone is not always about knowing how their story ends.
